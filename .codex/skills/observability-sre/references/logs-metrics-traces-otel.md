# Logs, Metrics, Traces and OpenTelemetry

Use structured telemetry with stable fields for service, environment, version/deployment, operation and correlation identifiers. Logs should describe significant state/outcome and errors without secrets or unnecessary personal data. Metrics should measure rates, latency distributions, errors, saturation, queue depth and domain-critical health with controlled cardinality. Traces should represent cross-service/request/job operations and preserve context across async boundaries when practical. Follow current OpenTelemetry semantic conventions where compatible so HTTP, database, messaging and other signals use consistent attributes. As of 2026 the semantic conventions are versioned independently and may include mixed stability; inspect instrumentation/library compatibility before mass-renaming telemetry. Sampling strategy must preserve enough failure/slow-path evidence to diagnose incidents while controlling cost.
