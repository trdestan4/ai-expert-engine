# Originality Evidence

Originality should be evidenced by design logic, not novelty claims.

Look for at least three of these:

- composition derived from actual content/product structure;
- category convention intentionally retained or challenged;
- distinctive brand asset used as a system, not decoration;
- typography behavior tied to brand/content needs;
- imagery language tied to product truth;
- meaningful signature interaction;
- unusual but credible content sequencing;
- responsive re-composition that preserves the same idea.

Weak evidence includes: uncommon hex values, random gradients, trendy 3D objects, unusual border radius, or a different font on a standard template.

If the design would still look identical after swapping logo/name/product images, originality is weak.
