# Creative Direction Framework

A creative direction is a system hypothesis, not a moodboard label.

For each direction define:

1. **Strategic idea** — one sentence tying product truth to visual expression.
2. **Emotional target** — what the user should feel and why that helps the product.
3. **Category codes** — what to preserve for comprehension/trust and what is overused.
4. **Composition** — density, grid, asymmetry, framing, scale contrast, section rhythm.
5. **Type character** — expressive vs neutral roles, hierarchy behavior.
6. **Color behavior** — temperature, saturation, accent discipline, surface hierarchy.
7. **Visual media** — photography/3D/illustration/material language.
8. **Motion character** — tempo, depth, continuity, signature moments.
9. **Signature device** — one or a few ownable visual/interaction ideas.
10. **Constraints** — mobile, accessibility, performance, content scalability.

## Comparison

Do not compare directions by taste alone. Test: fit, credibility, differentiation, scalability, accessibility, performance, content resilience, and implementation realism.

Directions must differ in concept/composition—not only palette, font, or background treatment.
