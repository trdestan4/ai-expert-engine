# Color Decision System

## Build in layers

1. **Intent:** recognition, calm, energy, product focus, editorial warmth, dense-app clarity, etc.
2. **Raw palette:** controlled hue families and tonal steps.
3. **Semantic roles:** canvas, surface, raised surface, primary/muted text, borders, action, focus, statuses.
4. **Interaction states:** hover, active, selected, disabled, destructive.
5. **Modes:** light/dark treated as separate luminance/chroma systems.

## Palette evaluation

Check category fit, differentiation, emotional temperature, image/product compatibility, contrast feasibility, and cultural context when material.

Neutrals can be warm/cool/chromatic; avoid assuming gray is always neutral. Accent should indicate brand/priority, not decorate every card/title.

Dark mode needs visible surface separation and often lower accent saturation. Product cutouts/photography may need tone/shadow/background treatment so they do not disappear.

Never justify a palette only with “premium,” “modern,” or industry convention.
