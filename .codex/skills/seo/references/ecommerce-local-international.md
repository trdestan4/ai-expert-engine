# Ecommerce / Local / International SEO

Ecommerce architecture should make category, subcategory, product and variant relationships understandable through URLs, breadcrumbs and internal links. Product pages need unique useful information, truthful price/availability, stable canonical behavior and intentional handling for filters/facets, pagination and out-of-stock/discontinued states. Coordinate Product/Offer/merchant markup with actual commerce data.

Local SEO needs accurate business identity, location/service-area information, contact data, locally useful pages and consistent structured signals; do not create doorway pages for every nearby city without real value.

International sites need deliberate locale/market URLs, reciprocal hreflang where applicable, correct canonical strategy and localized content rather than machine-swapped keywords. Market/currency/language switching must not make important versions uncrawlable.
