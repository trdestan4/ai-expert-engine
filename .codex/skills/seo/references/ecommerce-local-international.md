# Ecommerce, Local and International SEO

## Ecommerce/faceted navigation

Define which category/filter combinations deserve indexable landing pages based on demand/value/content uniqueness. Keep low-value parameter combinations crawlable/indexable only when intentional. Use canonical/internal linking/robots/noindex carefully; one universal `noindex all filters` rule can hide valuable pages, while indexing every combination creates crawl/index bloat.

Product lifecycle: decide out-of-stock vs discontinued behavior, alternatives, structured data and redirect/removal policy. Preserve useful product information when it serves users instead of redirecting every discontinued item to home/category.

Pagination/infinite scroll must expose crawlable navigation/content URLs where search discovery matters.

## Local

Location pages need actual location/service evidence, hours/contact, unique context and consistent business data. Avoid templated doorway pages for cities with no real presence/service distinction. Reviews/testimonials must be genuine and policy-compliant.

## International

Choose domain/subdomain/subdirectory strategy from business/operations, not ranking myths. Implement language/region alternates and hreflang reciprocity/canonicals correctly, preserve locale in internal links and avoid automatic geo redirects that block user/bot access.

Translate content and metadata meaningfully; do not rely on currency switch as localization. Test Unicode/slugs, language fallbacks and indexability per locale.
