# Content, Search Demand and AI-era Discoverability

## Search intent/content

Build content around user questions/jobs and evidence the organization can uniquely provide. Keyword volume is an input, not a content strategy. Map informational/commercial/navigational intent to page type and next step.

Avoid thin programmatic pages that merely swap keywords/locations. Programmatic content needs unique data, utility or decision support and strong quality controls.

## Content quality

Use subject-matter evidence, original examples/data, clear authorship/source where relevant, current maintenance and internal links. Remove/merge obsolete cannibalizing content when it improves user/search clarity rather than keeping pages for historic traffic alone.

## AI-generated content

Generation is acceptable only with factual/brand/editorial controls. Do not mass-publish hallucinated or derivative pages. Track source/provenance where useful, verify claims and ensure the page adds value beyond rephrasing existing search results.

## AI/answer-engine visibility

Optimize for clear entity facts, quotable/structured evidence, canonical source pages and crawl/index accessibility rather than speculative “LLM hacks.” Monitor referral/search behavior and verify platform-specific guidance before encoding it as a rule.

## Measurement

Use impressions/clicks/rank only with conversions/qualified engagement and page intent. Separate brand/non-brand, device/country/page-type where useful. SEO experiments are noisy; document seasonality, crawl/index lag and concurrent changes.
