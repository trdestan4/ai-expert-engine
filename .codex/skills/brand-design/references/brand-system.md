# Brand System Reference

A digital identity can include: mark/wordmark, color signature, typography behavior, graphic geometry, image treatment, icon/illustration style, layout signature, tone of motion, and verbal cues.

Do not require all elements. Choose the few that can build durable recognition.

## Logo checks

- recognizable at favicon/small size;
- legible in monochrome;
- not dependent on gradients/effects;
- avoids generic category symbolism unless conceptually transformed;
- has clear horizontal/compact behavior;
- survives light/dark contexts.

## Digital checks

Hide the logo temporarily. Does the interface still feel branded through type, color, composition, imagery, and graphic behavior? If not, the identity is logo-dependent.

Test with long pages, dense ecommerce/catalog screens, mobile, reduced color, and real content variability.
