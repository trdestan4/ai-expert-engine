# Visual Art Direction System

Create a visual bible covering only relevant media.

## Define

- asset jobs: evidence, atmosphere, explanation, category recognition, brand signature, decoration;
- dominant medium and supporting media;
- realism/stylization level;
- lighting and material behavior;
- camera/perspective or illustration geometry;
- crop, focal point, scale, negative space;
- background/environment;
- texture/finish/grain;
- UI color relationship;
- mobile/alternate crop rules.

## AI-generated asset consistency

Lock subject/product geometry where truth matters. Avoid fake text, plastic materials, impossible light/reflection, generic cinematic haze, excessive depth of field, random backgrounds, and inconsistent lens/perspective across a set.

For evidence-critical ecommerce/product views, prefer reliable source photography/renders when generative systems cannot guarantee accuracy.

## Production

Define responsive aspect ratios, focal preservation, transparent vs baked backgrounds, format/compression expectations, dark/light adaptation, alt text/caption needs, and performance budget.
