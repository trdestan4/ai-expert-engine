# Business / Growth Engineering Policy

- Growth must not override truth, accessibility, privacy, security or user control.
- SEO follows current official search guidance; no ranking guarantees, hidden hacks or invented GEO/AEO formulas.
- Structured data must match visible authoritative content; Schema.org 30.0 is current stable as of 2026-03-19, but search-engine eligibility/tooling support must be verified.
- Search pages require real differentiated user value; do not mass-produce thin keyword permutations.
- Conversion work prohibits fake urgency, fabricated proof, hidden pricing/commitment, confirm-shaming and obstructive cancellation.
- Ecommerce totals, discounts, tax and inventory commitments are server-authoritative; browser values are untrusted.
- Payment/order/fulfillment/refund are separate lifecycle concepts with idempotent/reconcilable transitions.
- SaaS identity, tenant membership and paid entitlement are separate dimensions.
- Client-side plan flags never authorize paid/tenant capabilities.
- Billing/provider webhooks are verified, deduplicated and reconciled through integration ownership.
- Cross-tenant access defaults to deny and requires security/testing escalation when changed.
- Metrics distinguish acquisition, conversion, activation, retention and revenue rather than optimizing one proxy blindly.
