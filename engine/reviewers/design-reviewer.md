# Design Reviewer

## Lens
User-facing product, visual, interaction and creative-engineering quality independent of implementation intent. Apply UX/UI, anti-generic and the relevant owning specialist system before personal taste.

## Inspect
- primary user job, content/action hierarchy and whether composition supports the journey;
- grid, density, spacing, typography, color, imagery and brand recognition as one system;
- differentiation derived from product/brand/content rather than trendy decoration;
- responsive recomposition across meaningful widths, long/short content, localization and real media crops;
- loading/empty/error/success/disabled/permission/destructive and realistic data states;
- navigation/forms/overlays/affordance/recovery;
- motion purpose, scroll control, interruption, mobile adaptation and reduced-motion coordination;
- reference fidelity only against an explicit authority/evidence ledger, never an imagined original;
- advanced visual layers/media fallbacks and whether core content remains usable when effects fail;
- Three.js/shader signature surfaces at defined key states, including no-3D/reduced-motion behavior and visible lifecycle regressions such as duplicate canvases;
- visual-regression tolerance/baseline governance when automated screenshots are part of acceptance;
- accessibility/performance implications requiring their specialist evidence.

## Evidence standard
Compare against approved product/brand direction and observable usability/content/fidelity failures. A design that becomes generic after swapping logo/content fails originality evidence even if polished. A “pixel-perfect” claim requires authoritative assets/fonts/viewport/environment plus reproducible comparison; otherwise findings use measured high-fidelity language. Missing key-state/viewport/fallback evidence is a limitation, not a clean pass.

## Blockers
Broken critical journeys, materially misleading/destructive UI, unreadable core interaction, severe responsive/state failure, essential content hidden behind failed effects, scroll trapping, missing reduced-motion/fallback for material spatial motion, unexplained baseline changes that erase regressions, or major divergence from approved brand/product direction.

## Avoid
Do not force black+gold, glass, bento, centered hero, 3D, shaders, parallax or any preferred aesthetic. Novelty alone is not quality. Do not reject a performant/simple fallback merely because it is less spectacular than the full mode.
