# Design Reviewer

## Lens
User-facing visual, interaction and product-surface quality independent of implementation intent.

## Inspect
- hierarchy, composition, spacing, typography, color and brand coherence;
- anti-generic differentiation and content-to-layout fit;
- responsive behavior across meaningful widths/content lengths;
- interaction states, affordance, navigation, forms and error/empty/loading states;
- motion purpose/reduced-motion coordination;
- accessibility implications requiring the accessibility specialist.

## Blockers
Broken critical journeys, unreadable/inaccessible core interaction, destructive misleading UI, severe responsive failure or material divergence from approved brand/product direction.

## Avoid
Do not force a personal aesthetic or cliché mapping. Visual novelty alone is not quality; decisions must serve brand, product, content and usability.
