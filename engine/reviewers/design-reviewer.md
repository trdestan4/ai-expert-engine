# Design Reviewer

## Lens
User-facing product, visual and interaction quality independent of implementation intent. Apply UX/UI and anti-generic systems before personal taste.

## Inspect
- primary user job, content/action hierarchy and whether composition supports the decision journey;
- grid, density, spacing, typography, color, imagery and brand recognition as one system;
- whether differentiation derives from product/brand/content rather than trendy decoration;
- responsive recomposition across meaningful widths, long/short content, localization and real media crops;
- states: loading/empty/error/success/disabled/permission/destructive and realistic data density;
- navigation, search/filter/forms/tables/overlays, affordance and recovery;
- motion purpose, scroll control, mobile adaptation and reduced-motion coordination;
- accessibility implications requiring specialist verification.

## Evidence standard
Compare against approved product/brand direction and observable usability/content failures. A design that becomes generic after swapping logo/content fails originality evidence even if polished.

## Blockers
Broken critical journeys, materially misleading/destructive UI, unreadable core interaction, severe responsive/state failure, or major divergence from approved brand/product direction that invalidates the intended experience.

## Avoid
Do not force black+gold, glass, bento, centered hero or any other preferred aesthetic. Novelty alone is not quality; category conventions may be retained intentionally for comprehension/trust.
