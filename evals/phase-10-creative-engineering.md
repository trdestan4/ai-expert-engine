# Phase 10 — Creative Engineering Evaluation Suite

## Routing positives
1. Screenshot/reference reconstruction routes to `design-reconstruction`, not straight to arbitrary CSS.
2. Advanced masks/layered responsive art direction routes to `creative-frontend`.
3. GSAP/ScrollTrigger implementation routes to `animation-engineering`; motion intent remains `motion-direction`.
4. Three.js scene/runtime/lifecycle routes to `threejs-webgl`; DCC/GLB optimization routes to `three-d-asset-pipeline`.
5. Custom GLSL/distortion/feedback routes to `realtime-shaders` only when standard material/CSS cannot own it.
6. Reference/regression fidelity routes to `visual-qa` after the implementation authority is defined.

## Routing negatives
- Ordinary component CSS should remain `frontend-engineering`.
- A request for “cinematic” direction alone should not auto-activate Three.js or GSAP.
- Normal PBR tuning should not auto-activate `realtime-shaders`.
- A huge GLB should not be “fixed” only in runtime if textures/topology/export are the cause.
- Visual regression must not replace functional, accessibility, or performance review.

## Edge cases
- One desktop screenshot: responsive rules are hypotheses, not observed facts.
- Scroll-triggered 3D: `animation-engineering` owns scroll timeline; `threejs-webgl` owns scene loop/resources; one property authority is defined.
- Reduced motion: remove pin-only geometry and spatial motion while preserving content order.
- Route revisit: no duplicate ScrollTriggers, requestAnimationFrame loops, canvases, listeners, GPU resources, or simulation targets.
- Failed WebGL/model/video load: semantic content/action remains available with designed fallback.
- Pixel-perfect claim with missing font/assets: system reports high-fidelity measurable deltas rather than unsupported exactness.

## Quality assertions
- Every Phase 10 skill has a narrow implementation/evidence boundary and two deep references.
- Material creative experiences can produce a schema-valid creative experience plan.
- 3D plans require lifecycle disposal and no-WebGL fallback.
- Signature moments are intentionally limited to avoid spectacle saturation.
- Responsive/mobile, reduced motion, capability fallback, performance budgets, and visual QA are first-class acceptance criteria.
- Official version-sensitive GSAP/Three.js/browser guidance is covered by knowledge freshness sources.
